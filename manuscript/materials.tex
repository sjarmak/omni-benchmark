The public dataset is LiveSQLBench Large-v1 at revision
\hash{a418e108d5cbb4cf9b783a928eff5e924ad2460d}
(\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/birdsql/livesqlbench-large-v1}), and the
official harness and evaluator are read at commit
\hash{e15cd221267e06fabfaf6a3d4a69308280ce9a7c}
(\url{https://github.com/bird-bench/livesqlbench}). The dataset card carries an
internal license inconsistency, CC-BY-4.0 in its front matter and CC-BY-SA-4.0 in
its footer, which is recorded here because it constrains redistribution of
derived public artifacts.

The preregistration artifacts required to reproduce the population and both
splits are the committed eligible manifest, train and test identifier lists,
manifest and split metadata, and the development split metadata, together with
the preparation and split scripts and the pinned seeds
\texttt{omni-livesqlbench-large-v1-split-v1} and
\texttt{omni-livesqlbench-large-v1-development-split-v1}. Their content hashes are
given in Section~\ref{sec:design}, and regeneration from the pinned source hash
is verified by test rather than asserted.

Private benchmark gold, hidden knowledge annotations, hidden test cases, run
artifacts, raw traces, and credentials are not redistributed and are excluded
from version control by construction: the custody tools never write held-out
records into the workspace, artifact validators reject protected fields
recursively at any nesting depth, and raw traces and generated SQL are confined
to ignored run roots. Aggregate results, hashes, counts, and permitted summaries
are the only correctness-adjacent material that appears in committed artifacts.

The executed result adds four classes of artifact, all identity-free. The
generation and score records for every attempt are hash-bound to each other and
to the Freeze-B manifest, with the commits and digests given in
Section~\ref{sec:design}. The deterministic telemetry aggregate behind
Table~\ref{tab:efficiency} hashes to
\hash{7a614d6c861d4d2a982501ea8c89b2817820d965671caf800cc155759be481a8}, and the
official aggregate receipt to
\hash{0296753e8fcbf826a99ed2f86088ecdfb61981db8dea47d93e7871cef2690a78}. The
frozen governed baseline's immutable selection hashes to
\hash{256145c13cfae7142d92f108b4ee9dd93e658a44cafb683e5aec90170b8315cc}, and an
append-only recovery manifest accounting for all 45 original capture failures, 11
result-only replays and 34 explicit evaluated-system failures, hashes to
\hash{5d6ff474f30d3de6d703ad5c6c59373fe8093515eabb83473bdb352c4f30fd9f}. The E02
candidate set hashes to
\hash{12c4e1a8cab38f0f47e14b5c553c87c800ca07f27bae568171f1d7caaf7589a7}, and its
no-rerun diagnostic is published as a separate aggregate artifact. The
repository's evidence index maps each claim in this paper to the artifact that
carries it.

The primary results report, the product-findings ledger, the failure taxonomy,
the governed query-path disclosure, and the contemporaneous research log all live
in the same public repository as this source. The query-path disclosure is the
one to read alongside Section~\ref{sec:conditions}: it lists every earlier
statement in the project that the measurement retracted, including the condition
table that described the governed arm as enforcing semantic compilation.

The comparison that motivated this ablation is the published benchmark of Omni's
agentic analytics harness
(\url{https://omni.co/blog/benchmarking-omnis-agentic-analytics-harness}). This
study tested that claim under a design that separates knowledge access,
representation, and enforcement. It did not attempt to reproduce it.
