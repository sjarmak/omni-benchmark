\section{Held-out results}
\label{sec:results}

The frozen comparison covers 89 held-out questions and 1,068 scheduled attempts:
four conditions, three repetitions, identical membership. Every generation
completed before any output was scored, and the scorer published both
preregistered policies in one atomic run. Everything below is derived from
identity-free aggregates. No question identity, SQL text, result row, hidden
annotation, or per-question correctness left custody to produce it.

comparative endpoint gives no evidence that the governed path outperformed the
direct baseline on these questions, and the interval is wide enough that it also
gives no evidence of a large loss.

Table~\ref{tab:matrix} carries a reliability result that the accuracy column
alone would hide. The governed condition is the most likely of the four to return
something rather than decline: it refused or errored on 14.2\% of scoreable
attempts against 33.7\% for C1 and 38.2\% for C3. It converted almost all of that
willingness into wrong answers, at a 77.2\% wrong rate against 56.2\% for C1. For
an analyst, a system that answers more often and is wrong more often is a
different product risk from one that declines more often, and the two are not
interchangeable at equal accuracy. The frozen generation contract does not
distinguish a content refusal from a statement that the available schema was
insufficient, so those two rates are reported as unavailable rather than
inferred.

Read as a ladder, Table~\ref{tab:contrasts} says one clear thing and one careful
thing.

The clear thing is that access to business knowledge is what moved the result.
Giving the same agent, the same harness, and the same retrieval budget a
searchable copy of the raw knowledge base is worth $+12.0$ points under the
official scorer and $+9.4$ under the corrected one, on 37 questions gained
against 5 lost. That contrast is the one rung where model, scaffold, dialect, and
execution contract are all held fixed, so it carries the most weight of any
comparison in the study.

The careful thing is that the two rungs after it did not keep the gain. C3 minus
C2 is $-13.5$ points, on 4 questions gained against 40 lost: converting the same
knowledge into the bounded structured model and exposing it through the same
search interface lost most of what the prose had provided. C4 minus C3 is
indistinguishable from zero. Neither of those is a verdict on semantic layers in
general, because both depend on how much of this knowledge base the compiler
could represent, and Table~\ref{tab:hkbcoverage} shows that it could represent 17.7\% of
it. The negative result is narrow, and worth stating
precisely: a conservatively compiled semantic model, built from these public
inputs and deployed without joins or measures, did not reproduce the benefit that
the same knowledge gave as searchable prose.

\subsection{Efficiency and operational reliability}

The same 1,068 immutable attempts preserve provider-reported token usage where
available, end-to-end latency, tool activity, and terminal state. These are
workload measurements of four complete systems, not estimates of the semantic
layer's isolated cost. Dollar cost is available for the direct provider surface
and unavailable for the governed product's raw job endpoint. Unavailable does not
mean zero, and it is recorded as null with a declared reason.

The governed condition spent 3.9 times C1's median tokens, 1.5 times its median
latency, and 2.3 times its median tool calls, and did not convert any of it into
accuracy. Almost all of the difference is input volume: median input and output
tokens were 145.8k and 2.0k for C1 against 580.0k and 3.0k for C4. Because
scaffold, agent, and execution path all differ between those two arms, the
overhead cannot be attributed to semantic enforcement, but the workload
difference itself is a measured product fact.

Failures cost more than answers in every condition. Median observed total tokens
for answered against errored attempts were 134k versus 335k in C1, 249k versus
367k in C2, 195k versus 403k in C3, and 574k versus 815k in C4, whose error-token
median uses the 36 of 38 errors that carried token telemetry. Governed error
latency was 87.5 seconds against 50.5 seconds for answered attempts. The product
opportunity there is not only fewer failures but earlier ones: a terminal
contract failure or an unsupported result type discovered at the end of an
expensive execution path is the worst place to discover it. Model version, retry
count, and validation-attempt count are unavailable for all 267 governed
attempts.

\subsection{What this result licenses}

Three statements are supported. Searchable business knowledge improved an
analytical agent's accuracy on these questions by a margin that survives both
scorers and both directions of the bootstrap interval. The governed
semantic-layer product, deployed from these public inputs, did not improve on the
raw-schema baseline and cost substantially more per attempt. And the governed
path failed differently rather than less: it declined far less often and was
wrong far more often.

Three statements are not supported, and the study should not be cited for them.
This is not a measurement of semantic-layer query composition, because no
governed query was composed by the semantic layer (Section~\ref{sec:conditions}).
It is not a general verdict on Omni, because the deployed model was built by a
conservative public-only compiler that published no joins and no measures, and a
model authored the way the product's documentation prescribes is a different
system. And it is not a full-benchmark result, because the executed frame covers
89 of the original 101 held-out questions on 16 of the 18 databases.

The second of those unsupported statements is the one a registered successor
condition is built to address. Section~\ref{sec:c5} defines C5, a governed
condition deployed the way the product's documentation prescribes rather than the
way a conservative compiler produces, fixes its comparisons in advance, and
carries the empty tables its result will fill. It is a development-partition
condition and reports nothing here.
