\section{Threats to validity and limitations}
\label{sec:threats}

Every item in this section except the last three was written down before any
result existed, which is why none of them can be introduced now as an explanation
for an inconvenient outcome. The last three are deviations the executed study
incurred, and they are stated at the same level of detail as the preregistered
ones.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item \textbf{Scope.} Because all 18 databases appear in both partitions, the
  headline estimates unseen-question performance inside a modeled database, not
  cold-start performance on an unmodeled one.
  \item \textbf{Supervision.} The protocol permitted offline use of training
  failures and training gold. The executed final candidate used neither, and the
  metered partition was never consumed, so the reported system is the frozen
  mechanical baseline rather than a tuned one.
  \item \textbf{Contamination.} The benchmark is public. The maintainers' design
  claim cannot rule out post-release model exposure, and this project cannot
  inspect training provenance. Paired within-question contrasts are less
  vulnerable than absolute levels, which is one reason the C2 minus C1 result
  carries more weight here than any single accuracy figure.
  \item \textbf{Power.} 89 held-out questions give roughly one percentage point
  of resolution per question. The knowledge contrast is large enough to clear
  that, at $+12.0$ points with a 5.6-point lower bound. The governed contrasts
  are not: C4 minus C1 at $-1.5$ points has an interval of $-7.1$ to 4.1, so this
  study cannot distinguish a small governed loss from a small governed gain.
  \item \textbf{Metric.} Soft EX deliberately equates some semantically different
  SQL and rejects some extensionally equal results. Accuracy is accuracy under a
  pinned scorer, not proof of semantic equivalence, which is why the second
  comparator is reported alongside it everywhere.
  \item \textbf{Model parity.} The governed condition's underlying models could
  not be matched or observed, so C4 minus C3 is a system-level comparison and not
  a causal estimate of enforcement.
  \item \textbf{Query path.} This limitation is separate from model parity and is
  not removed by achieving it. The governed condition never exercised semantic
  query composition: every governed query took the product's raw-SQL rewrite
  path, and the deployed model declared no join path and no measure for a planner
  to compile. C4 minus C3 therefore differs on the agent, on field resolution at
  rewrite time, on the accessible surface, and on the execution contract, and not
  on who composes the query.
  \item \textbf{Governed structural aggregates.} Because \texttt{generated\_sql}
  is null on every governed attempt, the governed arm's relation and join counts
  are computed from the SQL text carried on each semantic query. They describe
  agent-written queries in all four conditions and support no claim about
  compiled query structure.
  \item \textbf{Harness.} Published controlled studies place the scaffold effect
  anywhere from 0 to 28 percentage points~\cite{arxiv-2607-22585,arxiv-2606-08529},
  so all condition results are conditional on the disclosed harnesses, and effects
  may appear in error type, cost, retries, or reliability rather than in aggregate
  accuracy. The reliability and workload differences in
  Section~\ref{sec:results} are an instance of exactly that.
  \item \textbf{Comparator scaffold choice.} The four-table schema window is a
  comparator design decision supported by one question's evidence. The 20-question
  sensitivity arm that would test it was frozen and then deferred before
  execution.
  \item \textbf{Stochasticity.} Three repetitions characterize some instability
  but do not fully identify provider or temporal variance.
  \item \textbf{Environment parity.} Any mismatch between the database the
  evaluated product queries and the scorer's snapshot can manufacture false gains
  or false failures, which is why fingerprint and canary parity checks precede
  every run.
  \item \textbf{Version drift.} The product, the model providers, and the
  evolving benchmark can all change during an evaluation of this size. Pinned
  artifacts, timestamps, deterministic interleaving, and the sealed
  generate-then-score boundary mitigate this without eliminating it.
  \item \textbf{Scalability of the modeling claim.} Manual interpretive mappings
  and benchmark-specific exceptions weaken any claim that semantic-model
  construction is automatic, and are counted, not described qualitatively.
  \item \textbf{Composite systems.} A production system that routes across
  multiple opaque model tiers and includes a validation stage cannot be reduced to
  one model label, and is not reported as though it could.
  \item \textbf{Executed frame (deviation).} The held-out frame covers 89 of the
  original 101 sealed questions on 16 of the 18 databases. The 12 excluded
  questions sit on the two databases the official Linux loader cannot populate,
  and the exclusion was made before any sealed generation, label release, or
  outcome access, using only loader behavior. It narrows the estimand; it does
  not create missing outcomes. The same defect makes 18 development questions
  unscorable in the official environment. Loading the omitted archive files would
  restore those questions at the cost of comparability with the official
  environment, which is why it was not done.
  \item \textbf{Development-baseline scope (deviation).} The direct-SQL
  development baseline excludes two further databases and therefore describes
  those conditions on 16 databases. Any comparison against a wider governed frame
  must use matched coverage or disclose the mismatch; the alignment in
  Section~\ref{sec:development} does the former.
  \item \textbf{Scoring order (deviation).} The untuned held-out arm was scored
  before the E02 development experiment finished, reversing the intended order.
  The frozen untuned comparison is unaffected, because every held-out generation
  completed before scoring and both scorers were published together. The
  consequence is that no held-out claim about optimization can be made at all:
  E02 resolves inconclusive under its own coverage rule, and the visible held-out
  aggregates may not drive any further intervention, checkpoint, or promotion.
\end{itemize}
